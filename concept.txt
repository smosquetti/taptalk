
1. Leveraging Visual Strengths Over Auditory Processing

Many children on the autism spectrum process visual information much more effectively than spoken language.
• Visual-First Concept Mapping: Apps like the Stages Learning Language Builder utilize high-quality, customizable digital photo cards to pair new foreign words directly with concrete images. This removes the abstract nature of translation and anchors the vocabulary in reality.
• Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC): Dynamic AAC apps allow non-verbal or minimally verbal children to tap icons or photos to generate text-to-speech in the target language. This reduces immediate verbal pressure while simultaneously building receptive language skills (understanding the language) and expressive language skills (producing it).
